# Step 1: Process Discovery Planning

## Purpose

Process Discovery Planning establishes the scope and approach for understanding how the business operates before conducting the actual departmental discovery sessions.

The approved Business Requirements Specification (BRS) from Phase 1 was used as the starting point for determining which business functions should be investigated and how the discovery sessions would be conducted.

The objective was to create a practical plan for discovering the organization's processes without assuming the processes or cross-functional relationships in advance.

## Input from Phase 1: Approved BRS

The approved BRS identified eight business requirements:

| ID     | Business Requirement               |
| ------ | ---------------------------------- |
| BR-001 | Enterprise Performance Visibility  |
| BR-002 | Financial Performance Management   |
| BR-003 | Operational Performance Monitoring |
| BR-004 | Commercial Performance Management  |
| BR-005 | Project Performance Management     |
| BR-006 | Procurement & Supply Performance   |
| BR-007 | Workforce Performance              |
| BR-008 | Customer Experience Management     |

These requirements provided the basis for defining the process discovery scope.

**BR-001: Enterprise Performance Visibility** was treated as an enterprise-level requirement that cuts across the departmental discovery rather than as a separate departmental interview.

## Discovery Scope

The BRS was translated into the following departmental discovery scope:

| Department / Function | Relevant Requirement                        |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Finance               | BR-002 — Financial Performance Management   |
| Operations            | BR-003 — Operational Performance Monitoring |
| Sales                 | BR-004 — Commercial Performance Management  |
| Marketing             | BR-004 — Commercial Performance Management  |
| Project Management    | BR-005 — Project Performance Management     |
| Procurement           | BR-006 — Procurement & Supply Performance   |
| HR                    | BR-007 — Workforce Performance              |
| Customer Service      | BR-008 — Customer Experience Management     |

The scope identifies the areas to investigate; it does not define the actual processes. Those will be established through the discovery sessions.

## Interview Approach

Process discovery will be conducted through separate departmental interviews.

The main participants will be:

* Department heads / process owners
* Relevant subject matter experts

A consistent core structure will be used across the interviews, while department-specific questions will be derived from the relevant BRS requirement.

The purpose is to understand how activities are performed, how decisions are made, how information moves through the department, and how current reporting supports the business.

## Standard Discovery Structure

The same core discovery topics will be used across departmental interviews:

1. Department role & responsibilities
2. Main activities / processes
3. Process triggers and inputs
4. Activities, decisions & approvals
5. Outputs and handoffs
6. Other departments involved
7. Current reports & information used
8. How reporting is currently produced
9. Operational / reporting pain points
10. Information needed for decision-making
11. Open questions / follow-up

Department-specific questions will be added where required based on the relevant business requirement.
