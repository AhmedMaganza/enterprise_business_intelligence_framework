# Phase 2: Business Process Discovery
After completing Phase 1: Business Discovery & Requirements Gathering, the next step is to understand how the business actually operates.

Business requirements describe what the organization needs from BI. Process discovery investigates how the business performs the activities that generate those requirements and the information used to monitor them.

The purpose of this phase is to understand the key business processes, activities, inputs, outputs, decisions, handoffs, reporting practices, and cross-functional interactions across the organization.

The findings from this phase will provide the business-process context required for the next stage of the BI framework, where business processes will be translated into standardized KPIs and reporting requirements.

## Objectives

The objectives of Business Process Discovery are to:

* Understand the main processes performed across the business functions identified in the BRS.
* Identify key activities, decisions, approvals, inputs, and outputs.
* Understand how information moves between departments.
* Identify current reporting practices and information used by each function.
* Understand operational and reporting pain points.
* Identify information required to support business decisions.
* Establish a practical view of cross-functional business processes based on discussions with process owners and subject matters.

## Discovery Approach
Process discovery is being conducted through departmental discovery interviews with department heads, process owners, and relevant subject matters.

A consistent discovery structure is being used across departments while allowing department-specific questions to be derived from the relevant business requirements identified during Phase 1.

## Phase 2 Output

The outcome of this phase will be a practical understanding of the organization's business processes and how they generate the information required for performance management and decision-making.

This process understanding will provide the foundation for **Phase 3: KPI & Reporting Framework**, where business processes and requirements will be translated into standardized KPIs and reporting needs.
